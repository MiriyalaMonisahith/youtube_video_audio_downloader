========================================================================
⚡ VORTEX STUDIO - VIDEO DOWNLOADER & AI SHORTS GENERATOR
========================================================================

HOW TO RUN:

OPTION 1: Modern Web Studio (RECOMMENDED - Premium Glassmorphism UI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Run in your terminal:
    .\venv\Scripts\python web_server.py
Then open your browser at:
    http://localhost:5000


OPTION 2: Streamlit Dashboard UI
---------------------------------
Run in your terminal:
    .\venv\Scripts\streamlit run app.py
Then open your browser at:
    http://localhost:8501


OPTION 3: Interactive Launcher
------------------------------
Run:
    .\venv\Scripts\python run.py


OPTION 4: Command Line
-----------------------
- Download full video with quality (default 1080p):
    .\venv\Scripts\python Downloads_python/download_video.py "URL" 1080

- Download audio only (MP3 320kbps):
    .\venv\Scripts\python Downloads_python/download_video.py "URL" audio

- Generate AI Shorts from CLI:
    .\venv\Scripts\python generate_clips.py "URL"

========================================================================
FEATURES:
- Video Downloader: 4K, 2K, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p with estimated sizes.
- Audio Extractor: MP3 (320k, 256k, 192k, 128k), M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS.
- AI Shorts Generator: Whisper transcription, hook detection, 9:16 vertical crop, animated styled subtitles, viral titles & hashtags, ZIP download pack.
- Media Vault: In-browser playback and downloads library.
